## 2. Description of the App

Kitchen Scraps & Food Waste Quiz is an interactive educational application focused on composting and food waste reduction. The App includes:

- **Quiz System:** Timed multiple-choice quizzes across various food waste and composting categories
- **Daily Challenge:** A daily 3-question quiz with streak tracking
- **Scrap Directory:** A reference database of organic materials and their composting classifications
- **C:N Balance Calculator:** A tool for calculating carbon-to-nitrogen ratios in compost
- **Troubleshooting Matrix:** A diagnostic reference for common compost issues
- **Compost Health Audit:** An 8-question audit tool with grading and saved report history
- **Progress Analytics:** A dashboard for tracking your learning performance
- **Creator Suite:** A feature allowing content creators to submit custom quiz questions
- **Creator Directory:** A browsable collection of approved creator spotlights and their quizzes
- **Custom Quiz Builder:** A tool for creating personal quiz questions from JSON input
- **Bookmarks:** A system for saving questions with personal notes
- **Text-to-Speech:** Audio reading of quiz questions in 8 supported languages
- **Social Sharing:** Generate and share quiz results and achievements
- **Referral System:** Track referral sources for creator attribution
- **Virtual Credits:** An in-app virtual currency earned through gameplay
- **Theme Customization:** Multiple visual themes including Amber Gold
